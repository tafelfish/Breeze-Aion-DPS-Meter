## Aion 2 Meter 1.5.0

### Companion Interface

- Redesigned Companion with clearer navigation, a black and gray background, and light blue highlights.
- Reorganized menus into **Home, Encounters, Character, World and Settings**.
- Home displays your currently logged-in character, or your last logged-in character when the game is closed.
- Character resources can now be expanded directly on Home, including shared server resources.
- Reorganized Settings into searchable categories with automatic saving.

### Encounters & Log Storage

- Increased the encounter limit to **1,000,000 events**.
- Encounters that reach the limit are automatically saved as incomplete, allowing recording to continue for the next pull.
- Added a searchable, date-grouped encounter list showing your personal damage and DPS.
- Added encounter favourites and a **Favourites only** filter.
- Added automatic cleanup that keeps the **newest 500 encounters**, plus any older favourites. This is enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings.
- New encounter logs are automatically compressed without losing encounter details.
- Added an option to compress existing logs.
- Added a configurable encounter-log folder. Existing encounters move to the selected location, with favourites preserved.
- Cleaned up copied encounter images by removing interface instructions and the “You” label. Confirmed victories display **Cleared**.

### Home & Character Resources

- Added hover details showing whether resource values are recorded or estimated and when they were observed.
- Improved recognition of Odyle crafting snapshots while keeping character and shared server allowances separate.

### Notifications & Buff Reminders

- Added **Hide notifications during encounters** to suppress routine popups and their sounds while fighting.
- Skill reminders remain active, and suppressed notifications are not replayed after the encounter.
- Improved buff-state handling during dungeon transitions to reduce reminders caused by uncertain transition data.

### Pets & World

- Improved automatic pet-tracker recovery after capture interruptions when connection continuity and character identity can be confirmed.
- Corrected the alignment of selected pet spawn dots on the Interactive Map.
- Aligned boss countdowns into a consistent column, independent of location-name length.

### Compressing Existing Encounter Logs

1. Open **Companion → Settings → Data & support**.
2. Find **Encounter storage**.
3. Click **Compress existing encounters** and wait for the completion message.

Compression preserves encounter details and favourites. Compressed logs open normally in Companion without manual extraction. New logs are compressed automatically.

In the same section, you can disable **Keep the newest 500 encounters** or choose another encounter folder using **Browse**, then **Move existing encounters**.

**Favourite encounters you want to keep before automatic cleanup runs.** Cleanup runs after saving a new encounter; compressing existing logs does not trigger it.

### Known Issues

- Ketu and split-boss encounters in Abyssal Forge: Ludra may still occasionally fail to start recording. Further gameplay confirmation is needed.
- The reported WebView2 disconnection is still under investigation. Window-creation errors now provide clearer details.
- Very large encounters require more memory and can take longer to analyse.
