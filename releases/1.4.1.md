**Aion 2 Meter 1.4.1**

**Odyle & Dashboard**

- Added a dungeon-runs display to the separate Odyle window. Choose **40 or 80 Odyle per run** in Edit UI. It includes normal and charged energy and rounds down to the nearest half-run.
- Normal Odyle now catches up after the meter has been closed, using saved refill timing. Estimated balances stop at capacity and are replaced when fresh game data arrives.
- Added an estimated recharge countdown for EU characters before the first natural refill is observed.
- Improved live updates for daily Kinah and Emergency Supply progress.

**Buff Reminders**

- Added **My Debuff on Boss** tracking for supported skills, including duration, refreshes and expiration. Uncertain effects remain Unknown. **(Experimental)**

**Boss Timers**

- Bosses reported as spawned now appear first, followed by respawn timers from shortest to longest. Stale or unavailable timers appear last.

**Encounter Tracking**

- Fixed lingering damage-over-time ticks after a wipe or reset starting another encounter and inflating the next pull’s duration. Damage-over-time during an active encounter continues to count normally.

**Known Issues & Limitations**

- The pet tracker may disappear around Eternal Isle or after travelling to the opposing faction’s territory, even while farming. This remains under investigation.
- Dashboard counters can have different observation times. A recent character update does not necessarily mean every counter is current.
- Estimated Odyle assumes no unobserved spending. Accuracy around dungeon transitions, spending and refill boundaries remains under investigation; fresh game data replaces estimates.
- Boss debuff tracking supports only effects that can be reliably identified. Unsupported effects and temporarily unavailable observations may both appear as Unknown.
