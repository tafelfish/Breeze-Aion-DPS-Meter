## 1.5.2

**Ludra Hard Mode**

- Fixed missing boss recognition for Rahu, Ketu, the central reactor and Eternal Ludra.
- Added an Estimated ten-minute enrage countdown for hard-mode Eternal Ludra.

**Character Management**

- Added Manage characters to Home and Character.
- Hide characters from character lists and resource windows, including Odyle. Hidden characters stay hidden after logging in again and can be restored at any time.
- Remove a saved character to clear its resource card. The character is added again when detected on a later login.
- Hiding or removing characters preserves encounter logs, shared resources, live pet tracking and buff reminders.

**Buff Reminders**

- Added a per-skill option to display buff duration and cooldown together on one icon, labelled B and CD.
- Sound warnings remain tied to missing selected buffs when entering a dungeon. Buff expiry and cooldown completion stay silent.

**Ping and Connection Status**

- Improved ping handling during delayed acknowledgements and connection interruptions.
- Stopped stale ping readings from remaining on screen.
- Preserved brief latency spikes in the 60-second history.
- Added an Unstable indicator for retransmissions, traffic gaps and stalled acknowledgements.
- Added a Waiting timer while outgoing game data remains unacknowledged, with separate history markers for measured ping and waiting time.

**Merchant Window**

- Prevented overlapping merchant-window creation attempts.
- Added automatic retries when the merchant window fails to open.
- Improved troubleshooting details for window-creation failures.

**Known Issues and Limitations**

- Ludra hard-mode recognition still needs live confirmation. Its enrage countdown is Estimated and may start late when joining an ongoing fight.
- Ping measures connection acknowledgement time. Server processing delays and frame-rate problems may occur without high ping. Waiting is a minimum observed delay, not a completed ping measurement.
- WebView2 disconnections can still prevent overlays from opening. Merchant-window recovery improvements do not yet resolve every reported case.
- The reported DPS-meter hide-shortcut issue remains under investigation.
