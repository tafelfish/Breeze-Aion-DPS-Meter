- **Edit UI**
  - Added a dedicated menu for adjusting window positions, sizes, colors and layouts.
  - The actual meter, pet tracker, merchant window and skill reminders display sample data while editing.
  - Preview ten damage-meter players, sample pets and between 1 and 20 skill icons.
  - Previews open on the game’s monitor, with a display selector available.
  - **Save & exit** applies changes; **Cancel** restores the previous layout.

- **Buff reminders**
  - Added character-specific selection of supported Active, Passive and Stigma skills.
  - Selected skills appear as compact icons with countdowns inside. Inactive icons remain visible and dimmed.
  - Choose **Buff duration** or **Skill cooldown** for each skill, with independent countdown and optional blinking settings.
  - Added horizontal and vertical layouts with adjustable icon sizes.
  - Added visibility options: **Always show**, **Only when Aion 2 is running**, **Only when Aion 2 is focused**, and **During Boss Encounters**.
  - Added optional dungeon-entry reminders. After a ten-second grace period, missing selected buffs trigger one sound and blinking icons. These reminders stay hidden outside dungeons and disappear when activated.
  - Per-skill options are grouped inside each selected skill’s card.

- **Black Cloud Merchant**
  - Added a separate window showing unfinished pets offered in the Special and Pets tabs.
  - Displays pet icons, ranks, remaining souls, quantities and prices.
  - Added **Unfarmable** tags and a saved **Unfarmable Pets only** filter.
  - Window size and position are saved.

- **Pet tracking and interactive map**
  - Nearby pets can display approximate distances in meters while both position readings are fresh. Distances disappear when readings become outdated.
  - Added tracker visibility options: **Always show** and **Only when Aion 2 is focused**.
  - Added a configurable shortcut to hide or show the nearby pet tracker while tracking continues.
  - Completed pets automatically mark their verified locations as completed on the interactive map. **Hide completed** also hides these pins.
  - Added faction tags and indicators for pets mapped in both factions.
  - Improved tracker recovery when returning to unfinished pets and resuming farming.

- **DPS meter**
  - Fixed an empty window appearing in **During Boss Encounters** mode.
  - Minimizing the meter temporarily keeps it hidden, including when returning focus to the game. Restore it through Companion, the tray or its shortcut.

- **Notifications and default settings**
  - Simultaneous notifications receive individual toast bubbles. Up to three appear together, with additional notifications queued individually.
  - Boss notifications are disabled by default for new settings.
  - The DPS meter, skill reminders and pet tracker default to **Only when Aion 2 is focused** for new settings.
  - Existing settings, favorites and custom reminder times are preserved.

- **Capture setup**
  - Added a clear warning when Npcap is missing or unavailable, with a link to the official installer, instead of leaving the app at **Waiting for login**.

- **Known issues**
  - Missing merchant offers when switching shop tabs remain under investigation.
  - Automatic merchant-window closing when the in-game shop closes is not yet supported.
  - Reported intermittent pet tracker disappearance still needs further testing.
