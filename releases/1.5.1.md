## Aion 2 Meter 1.5.1

### DPS Meter & Enrage Timers

- Added enrage countdowns for supported Sanctuary raid bosses: **10 minutes for Eternal Ludra** and **8 minutes for Kaldrix and Terminator Bargott**.
- Valid boss-specific deadlines take priority. Fallback countdowns are labelled **Estimated**.
- Added a darker section of the boss HP bar showing the health estimated to remain when enrage begins.
- Time to Kill turns red when it exceeds the remaining enrage time.
- Added **Boss HP percentage** and **Enrage timer** options in Edit UI, available separately for anchored and unanchored modes on the main and additional meters.
- Hiding the enrage display also hides its HP projection and enrage-based TTK warning.

### Encounter Recording

- Improved reset detection to prevent damage from a previous attempt carrying into a new pull.
- Improved encounter continuity during in-place resurrection and repeated updates for the same living boss.
- Prevented lingering damage-over-time ticks and delayed damage from starting a new pull after a detected reset.
- Increased capacity for distinct combat sources and improved entity cleanup during long sessions.
- Unknown damage ownership remains unassigned.
- Prevented previous encounters’ status-history limit warnings from carrying into new encounters.

### Healing

- Improved preservation of valid party HP data when an unrelated character identity update cannot be resolved, allowing more reliable healing matching. **(Experimental)**
- Healing that cannot be calculated reliably remains marked as unknown.

### Performance & Stability

- Reduced memory pressure and unnecessary updates in the DPS meter and additional meters.
- Reduced unnecessary memory use when changing meter settings and displaying buff reminders.
- Added automatic recovery for crashed meter displays while preserving the active encounter.
- Improved shutdown handling through Companion and the system tray, including clearer save errors and stopping background recovery during shutdown.

### Known Issues

- Ketu and split-boss Ludra encounters may still occasionally fail to start recording.
- The encounter reset and in-place resurrection improvements still need further live gameplay confirmation.
- Estimated enrage countdowns can be late when recording starts after the pull. HP projections can change with mechanics, downtime and damage output.
- The original cause of the reported large memory spike remains under investigation.
