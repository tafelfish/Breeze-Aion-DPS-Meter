- Added a Buffs view to the live meter and encounter logs, showing each player's confirmed team buff uptime.
- Added individual buff breakdowns and timelines, including given, received and self buffs.
- Added descriptions for known buffs, with applying skills and recorded skill levels where available.
- Made the Encounters list collapsible, with its state remembered.
- Added an optional meter visibility mode that fades in for boss encounters and fades out 60 seconds after they end. A new pull cancels the countdown, and capture and logging continue while hidden.
- Added character-name search to encounter logs. History defaults to the currently detected character.
- Added a Pets page with automatic login sync for pet ranks and soul progress.
- Added pet search, genus filters, favorites, recent soul gains, map links and an option to hide completed pets. Progress and preferences are saved per character.
- Added pet completion totals for each faction's lands, counting shared pets in both totals, and saved location filters to find unfinished pets only mapped on the other side.
- Added a compact nearby pet progress window showing pet portraits, rank progress and remaining souls, with its position and size remembered.
- Fully completed pets briefly glow gold in the progress window and play a chimes sound, which can be disabled in Pets.
- Pet rows keep a stable order, with favorites first, followed by name.
- Nearby pets disappear after one minute without a fresh sighting or farming progress. Fixed expired entries returning after delayed soul gains.
- Added scheduled reminders for Shugo Festival, Dimensional Invasions and Spacetime Rifts, with configurable schedules, timezones and advance warnings.
- Event reminders are enabled by default, with warnings 5 minutes before and at the start. Default schedules use Europe/Berlin: Shugo Festival every hour from 00:00, Dimensional Invasions every 2 hours from 00:30, and Spacetime Rifts every 3 hours from 02:00.
- Event reminders use the configured alert appearance, position and sound, even while the meter is hidden.
- Fixed an issue that could leave combat power and item level missing when a party member visits another instance. Still needs more testing.
- Fixed an issue where unreadable combat records could stop nearby pet tracking.
