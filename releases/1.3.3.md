**Aion 2 Companion 1.3.3**

**Crafting Tree (Experimental)**

- Fixed missing equipment prerequisites in 76 higher-tier weapon, armour and accessory recipes across both factions.
- Updated material requirements and costs to include every required crafting branch.
- Owned equipment correctly reduces the remaining requirements without being deducted twice.
- Corrected six faction-specific material mismatches.
- Prevented marketplace estimates from replacing complete recipes with incomplete crafting chains.

**Ping Window**

- Fixed the separate ping window jumping from the bottom edge to the top-left when restoring layouts or cancelling Edit UI.
- Preserved positions within the taskbar area, including layouts using top or left taskbars.

**Interface**

- Standardised window controls and shortcut labels to **Toggle Clickthrough**.
- Appearance modes now use **Clickthrough On** and **Clickthrough Off**.

**Known Issue**

- Skill reminders may trigger incorrectly when entering a dungeon while the skill’s buff is already active.
