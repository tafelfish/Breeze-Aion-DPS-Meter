**Aion 2 Companion — Version 1.3.2**

**Damage Tracking**

- Improved summon damage tracking, including Divine Aura and Spiritmaster summons.
- Recover recent summon hits when spawn or owner information arrives late.
- Prevent unidentified spirit attacks from appearing as separate players.
- Improved tracking of opening hits when boss identification arrives late.
- Fixed missing boss damage when the game reuses a previous player’s entity ID.
- Prevent duplicate counting and incorrect attribution after despawns or connection changes. Uncertain ownership remains excluded.

**Memory & Performance**

- Saved encounter details now load when needed instead of keeping every old fight in memory. Existing saved encounters remain intact.
- Reduced repeated calculations and unnecessary updates between windows.
- Live Companion analysis combines rapid updates into fewer refreshes while keeping normal meter updates responsive.
- Hidden and minimized windows skip unnecessary updates and refresh when reopened.
- Unused overlay windows are created on first use, preserving saved layouts, visibility and anchor settings.

**Meter Display & Settings**

- Healing meters can display **total restored HP → HPS → share %**. **(Experimental)**
- Total, rate and percentage settings now use the selected metric’s terminology: damage, healing, damage taken or buffs.
- Corrected labels across main and additional meters, including anchored and unanchored appearance settings.
- Healing continues to exclude overhealing and uncertain events.

**Known Issues**

- Skill reminders may trigger incorrectly when entering a dungeon, even when the skill’s buff is already active.
