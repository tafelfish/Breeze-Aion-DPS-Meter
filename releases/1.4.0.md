**Aion 2 Companion 1.4.0**

**Dashboard**

- Added a Dashboard with compact character cards showing portraits, class, item level, combat power and Odyle Energy.
- View shared resources and character-specific activities, including dungeon entries, keys, Supply missions, Kinah earned, Abyss time and weekly Odyle crafting and purchases.
- Character information is saved between sessions, with **Last recorded**, **Estimated** and **Unknown** labels indicating its reliability.
- Added a Season Missions overview with category filters and supported recorded progress and reward states. Tracking coverage is still incomplete. **(Experimental)**

*Log into each character to collect their information. Login, relogging, teleports and dungeon transitions can refresh supported values when the game sends updates. Some counters require opening the corresponding menus.*

**Odyle Energy & Refill Warnings**

- Track normal and charged Odyle Energy for each character, with automatic capacity detection from available membership information.
- View estimated recharge timing and time until full after the meter observes a natural refill. The schedule is saved through teleports and restarts.
- Added capacity and overflow warnings for normal Odyle Energy and Nightmare tickets.
- Added estimated weekly resets for supported entry, crafting and purchase counters.

**Odyle Overview Window**

- Added a transparent horizontal overview displaying character names, class icons and Odyle balances.
- Supports automatic sizing, custom character order, dragging, locking and appearance settings through Edit UI.
- Shows only while Aion 2 is focused by default, with configurable visibility.

**Dungeon Loot (Experimental)**

- Added a combined overview of observed dungeon loot across your characters.
- Compare item quantities by rarity and expand each rarity to inspect individual items.
- Includes saved loot history with recognised item and dungeon names.
- Excludes Kina, Unique Ariel Fragments and Unique Amplify Stone Fragments.
- Incomplete samples show observed item shares rather than claiming reliable drop rates.

**DPS Meter**

- Added player skill breakdowns directly inside the main and additional meters.
- Ranked skill bars show each skill’s contribution, with Damage, Healing and Tanked tabs and a Back button.
- The boss summary is hidden while inspecting skills to provide more space.
- Inline healing breakdowns show verified restored HP, excluding overhealing and keeping unclear measurements unknown. **(Experimental)**
- Hovering over a meter pauses its post-encounter hide countdown.
- Scarecrow encounters continue through zero HP and HP resets, ending after more than 30 seconds without qualifying damage.
- Improved class identification to prevent incorrect player bar colours and icons.

**Pets & Interactive Map**

- Selecting supported pets reveals highlighted recorded spawn locations. Closing their details restores the area marker.
- Improved pet-tracker continuity during collection refreshes and character transitions.
- Improved tracker-window visibility reliability.

**Interface**

- Reorganised Companion navigation into coloured groups.
- Reduced unnecessary Edit UI window updates.
- Improved handling of slow editing operations, cancellation and delayed save responses.
