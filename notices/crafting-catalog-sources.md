# Crafting catalog and artwork

Recipes and item-to-icon mappings reference [xtopolis/aion2-static](https://github.com/xtopolis/aion2-static), using its Global LST snapshot dated 2026-09-21. Its MIT notice is included in crafting-catalog-MIT.txt. Game artwork remains the property of its respective rights holders; the software license does not transfer ownership of that artwork.

Supported success-rate curves reference [Metabot crafting data](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/crafting) and its [methodology](https://metabot.gg/en/aion-2/methodology), retrieved on 2026-10-21. Recipe rates are matched by recipe and item identifiers. Missing prices and unsupported rates remain unknown. These estimates are not guarantees for a player's current proficiency or complete market costs.
